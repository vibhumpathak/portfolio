About Me

Getting to know me

A closer look at who I am, what I care about, and where I'm headed.

Who I Am

My name is Vibhum Pathak, and I am a student at Lebanon Trail High School. I have always been someone who likes to understand how things work. Whether it is technology, a new idea, or a difficult problem, I enjoy figuring out how different pieces fit together.

I would describe myself as curious, ambitious, and willing to take on challenges. When I become interested in something, I like to learn as much as I can about it.

My Interests

I am particularly interested in artificial intelligence, computers, engineering, and emerging technology. I enjoy learning about new developments and thinking about how they could be used to solve real-world problems — from autonomous systems to the tools we use every day.

My Activities

Outside of academics, I'm involved in Boy Scouts of America through Troop 416, where I've held troop leadership positions and am working toward Eagle Scout. Scouting has shaped how I think about responsibility, service, and working with others.

I also compete in debate, including Congressional Debate. Between tournaments and outreach to middle-school students, debate has sharpened my research, public speaking, and quick thinking.

What Drives Me

I believe that making mistakes is part of learning. Trying something, finding what does not work, and improving it is often how the best ideas develop. I'd rather understand technology than just use it — that's what keeps me curious.

My Goals

I want to continue developing my technical, leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills while exploring careers involving technology and engineering.

My Mission

"I want to continue learning, challenge myself, and use technology and engineering to create smarter, safer, and more effective solutions."

What ISM Is

Independent Study and Mentorship gives me the opportunity to explore a topic in greater depth through independent learning, research, professional guidance, and hands-on work.

My Topic

Artificial Intelligence and Computer Technology

My interests within this topic include how AI systems are developed, how AI is applied across different industries, how technology affects everyday life, and where the field is headed next.

Why I Chose This Topic

Technology has always been one of my biggest interests. I enjoy understanding how computers work and learning about new technologies. Artificial intelligence interests me because it combines areas I enjoy, including computers, mathematics, engineering, and problem-solving. AI is also developing quickly, which makes it an interesting field to explore right now.
